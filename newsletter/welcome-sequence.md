## Email 3 — Real Workflow + BA Assistant (send ~5 days in)

**Subject:** How I actually use AI as a Product Owner

**Body:**

Hey,

Here's my real AI workflow. Nothing fancy.

I come out of a stakeholder meeting with rough notes. Half-decisions. Open questions. A few "we'll confirm later" points.

**Before:** An hour turning that into requirements.

**Now:** I paste the notes and ask:

> "Convert these meeting notes into functional requirements, NFRs, user stories with Given-When-Then acceptance criteria, risks, assumptions, and open questions."

Example —

Input: *"Customer should be able to upload KYC documents and track status. Admin needs approval flow. Notifications required."*

Output:
- **Requirement:** Customer can upload PAN, Aadhaar, and address proof
- **User story:** As a customer, I want to track my application status so I know what action is pending
- **Risk:** Document verification failure may block onboarding
- **Open question:** Which KYC vendor API are we integrating with?

Then I review. That part matters. AI gives the first draft. I add product context, remove wrong assumptions, and share the cleaned version with the team.

I use the same flow for backlog refinement, diagramming, and spec writing.

BA Assistant automates this entire flow in one click — requirements, user stories, diagrams, risk assessment, and export-ready report.
