A bit about me

Product Owner by day, builder by night.

I'm a Product Owner at Broadridge, working in fintech for over 12 years. I spend my days turning complex business requirements into working software.

At night, I build AI tools. It started with a simple question: "Can AI help me do requirement analysis faster?" That led to BA Assistant — a multi-agent system that now does in 60 seconds what used to take days.

Since then, I've been building more — writing tools, LLM evaluation toolkits, stock screeners. Each one solves a real problem I've faced in my own work. This site is where I share them, for free.

I believe the best tools come from people who live the problem. If you're a BA, a PM, or anyone who builds software — I hope something here makes your work a little easier.
