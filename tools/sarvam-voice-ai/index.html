Voice AI

Sarvam Voice AI Toolkit

Multilingual Voice Agent & TTS Studio · Live

A free, open-source Streamlit app that brings Sarvam AI's voice technology to your browser. Record your voice, converse with AI, generate speech in 11 Indian languages — all from one tool.

🎙️ Launch Voice Agent → 📝 Launch STT Studio → 🐙 View Source

Three tools, one repo

🎙️

Conversational Voice Agent

Record your voice or type a message. The agent transcribes your speech, generates an AI reply, and speaks it back to you — all in your chosen Indian language.

  • Voice input via browser microphone
  • Speech-to-text in 11 Indian languages
  • AI-powered conversation (Sarvam LLM)
  • Text-to-speech reply with 7 voice options
  • Download conversation audio
Launch →
📝

STT Studio

Upload WAV audio files and get accurate transcriptions in 11 Indian languages. Preview audio, download transcripts, and inspect raw JSON responses.

  • Upload WAV files for transcription
  • Audio preview before processing
  • 11 Indian languages + English
  • Multiple STT modes (transcribe, translate, verbatim, codemix)
  • Download transcripts as .txt
  • View raw JSON response + event log
Launch →
🔊

TTS Studio

Convert any text to natural speech with 7 different Indian voices. Compare side-by-side, adjust pace, and download audio files.

  • 7 Indian voices (male + female)
  • 11 Indian languages + English
  • Adjustable speech pace
  • Side-by-side voice comparison
  • Download as MP3 or WAV
