🧑‍💻 About

Touseef Shaik

Product Owner · AI Builder · 12+ years in product and technology

I'm a Product Owner in fintech, where I spend my days translating complex stakeholder requirements into clear, delivery-ready specs for engineering teams. Over 12+ years across product and technology delivery, I've seen every kind of requirements mess — vague emails, contradictory stakeholder notes, thousand-line PRDs with no structure, and slide decks pretending to be specs.

I started building BA Assistant because I was tired of spending hours structuring requirements manually. What began as a personal productivity tool evolved into a 5-agent AI workflow that Business Analysts, Product Owners, and engineering leads now use to turn raw input into structured deliverables in under 60 seconds.

Every tool on this site comes from a real workflow pain I've lived — not from theoretical product-market-fit exercises. I believe the best tools are built by people who understand the problem deeply and use their own product daily.

What I believe

AI should make product people faster, not replace them. The best AI tool for a BA or Product Owner is one that takes the repetitive, structural work off their plate — structuring, categorising, diagramming — so they can focus on judgment, strategy, and stakeholder conversations.

I'm building practical, focused tools for a specific audience — not chasing AGI or building a platform for everyone. If you're a BA, PO, PM, or product team member who wants to move faster from messy input to clear output, I'm building for you.
