What Stock Research Assistant Does

Stock Research Assistant is an AI-powered equity research tool for the Indian stock market. It takes any NSE/BSE ticker and produces a structured, multi-lens research report with a clear verdict — Hold, Buy, Avoid, or Watchlist. Deep-research mode runs 10 specialist modules and renders the output as an institutional-grade PDF.

Standard mode gives a quick 4-lens snapshot in under 30 seconds. Deep-research mode runs the full pipeline in 60-90 seconds and produces a 9-page PDF report with a verdict panel, KPI boxes, business-model tables, forecast snapshots, valuation scenarios, risks, catalysts, governance check, and a monitoring dashboard.

The 4 Lenses

  1. Fundamentals — Profitability, balance-sheet strength, cash flows, growth trajectory, and Screener.in financial snapshot.
  2. Technicals — Trend, moving averages, support/resistance, volume profile, and momentum indicators.
  3. Sentiment — News tone, analyst ratings, social signals, and earnings-concall sentiment.
  4. Risk — Red flags, governance concerns, leverage, promoter pledging, and peer-relative risk score.

Deep-Research Modules (10 Agents)

Deep-research mode coordinates 10 specialist modules, each powered by DeepSeek:

  1. Valuation Engine — DCF, comparable-company, and graham-margin estimates with fair-value ranges.
  2. Financial Trends — Revenue, EBITDA, PAT, ROE, and cash-flow trend analysis across recent periods.
  3. Investment Thesis — Bull, bear, and base-case arguments synthesized into a single coherent thesis.
  4. Screener Client — Pulls key financial ratios and metrics from Screener.in for quick triage.
  5. Peer Analysis — Compares the stock against sector peers on valuation, margins, and returns.
  6. Risk Flags — Identifies accounting, governance, debt, and market risks with severity scoring.
  7. Governance Check — Promoter holding, pledge, auditor notes, and related-party observations.
  8. Sentiment Agent — News, analyst views, and concall tone summarised into a sentiment score.
  9. Catalyst Tracker — Upcoming events, earnings dates, sector tailwinds, and potential triggers.
  10. Report Builder — Compiles everything into a clean, institutional-style PDF report with charts and tables.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which markets does it cover?

NSE and BSE-listed stocks via yfinance tickers. Use the .NS suffix for NSE, e.g. RELIANCE.NS, INFY.NS, SBIN.NS.

Can it place trades?

No. Stock Research Assistant is research-only. It never connects to a broker or executes orders.

Where does the data come from?

Public market data via yfinance and Screener.in. News and sentiment are aggregated from available web sources. Always cross-check before making investment decisions.

What's the difference between Standard and Deep-Research modes?

Standard mode gives a quick 4-lens snapshot. Deep-research mode runs the full 10-module pipeline and produces a downloadable institutional PDF report with valuation scenarios and governance checks.

Can I export the report?

Deep-research mode exports a branded PDF. Standard mode gives a text summary you can copy.

Is this investment advice?

No. It is a research assistant. The final verdict is an AI-generated synthesis, not a recommendation to buy or sell. Always consult a SEBI-registered advisor if needed.
