Privacy Policy

How we handle your data and privacy

Last updated: May 2026

What data we collect

This website (touseefshaik.com) is a static site. We do not use cookies, tracking scripts, or analytics beyond a simple anonymous page-view counter. When you visit:

  • We do not collect your name, email, IP address, or any personal information.
  • A visitor counter badge (visitor-badge.laobi.icu) increments anonymously — no personal data is stored.

BA Assistant & AI tools

When you use BA Assistant or other AI tools hosted on Streamlit Cloud:

  • The requirements or text you paste are sent to the AI model provider (DeepSeek) for processing.
  • Free users: We do not store, log, or retain your input or output on our servers. Each session is stateless.
  • Pro users: Report history is stored only when you create an account and explicitly save a report. You can view, export, or delete your saved reports at any time from your account dashboard.
  • Do not paste confidential, proprietary, or personally identifiable information (PII).
  • AI-generated outputs should be reviewed by a human before use in production.

Third-party services

This site links to external platforms (GitHub, LinkedIn, Streamlit Cloud). Their privacy policies apply when you visit those sites.
