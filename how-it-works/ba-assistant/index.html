The Agents

Five specialized agents collaborate on every analysis. Each handles one dimension of the BA/PO workflow:

1. BA Requirements Analyst

Structures raw business needs into clear, categorized functional & non-functional requirements. Extracts entities, business rules, and constraints.

DeepSeek V4 Flash

2. Product Owner Analyst

Converts requirements into epics, features, and user stories. Applies MoSCoW prioritization and creates backlog structure.

DeepSeek V4 Flash

3. Solution Architect

Designs technology stack, integration patterns, data models, and NFRs. Recommends architecture patterns and deployment strategies.

DeepSeek V4 Flash

4. Risk & QA Reviewer

Identifies technical, business, and project risks. Validates completeness and consistency across all agent outputs.

DeepSeek V4 Flash

5. Mermaid Diagram Designer

Generates process flows, sequence diagrams, state charts, and data flow diagrams directly from requirements — ready to paste into any markdown document.

DeepSeek V4 Flash

How a Request Flows

1. User pastes requirements →
2. BA Analyst structures them →
3. PO Analyst extracts features & stories →
4. Architect designs the system →
5. Risk & QA reviews everything →
6. Diagram Designer generates Mermaid charts →
7. Consolidated report delivered to user
